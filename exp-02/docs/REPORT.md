* Preconditions: **PASS** (calibrated Google row #0 passed=True, SHARCNet=BLOCKED).
* R3 at matched cost: **FAIL**.
* Bayes-R3 at matched cost (#4): **FAIL**.
* MG lockstep (Soft-MG): **PASS**.

* R3+MG Large Sequence: **PASS**.
* Overhead: **PASS** (max 1.00x deterministic).

**Decision: GO for the calibrated baseline + Soft-MG package; NO-GO for the matched-cost R3/Bayes-R3 claim.**  The M_i > l_i calibration (D=1e-4) reproduces the paper's Google baseline (latency +2.05%, cost -88.89%, M>l for every service), which unblocks the pipeline.  Soft-MG (tau=0.15) keeps the cyclic lockstep gain (59-79% lower latency, p<0.05 over 30 seeds) while reducing the Google cost regression to 0.00% (row #6 PASS).  R3 and Bayes-R3 still do not beat deterministic Online-DRL at matched cost (row #4 FAIL), so the paper is positioned as an empirical MG/calibration result with a negative matched-cost result; the O(log k) claim remains a conjecture.
