**Decision: NO-GO for the claim that R3+MG improves on Online-DRL.**  The baseline precondition fails (the bundled Google traces do not reproduce the paper's published latency/cost split, and SHARCNet is unavailable), and R3 does not win at matched cost.  MG does break lockstep on the cyclic family (59-79% lower latency, p<0.05 over 30 seeds) and R3+MG lowers the Large Sequence ratio at every k, but both results are formally blocked by the baseline precondition, and MG regresses cost by more than 2% on the natural-crossing Google traces.  The isolated ski-rental ratio, the MG lockstep result and the Large Sequence slope are positive signals, not end-to-end evidence.
