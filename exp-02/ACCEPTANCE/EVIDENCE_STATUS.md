# What the current evidence supports

This is the status **before** the exp-02 runs.  It is kept as the reference
point; `docs/REPORT.md` records the post-run status.

* **Accepted:** H0 reduction, the threshold mean, and the ski-rental ratio of
  1.58.
* **Promising but unconfirmed:** MG on the cyclic adversary.  It is one
  synthetic workload with a custom tie-break.
* **Not supported:** R3 helping at matched cost.  The demo showed a 5–7% cost
  increase at equal `lam`, and the Large Sequence gain was only about 8%.

A claim like "R3+MG is better than Online-DRL" should only be written once the
preconditions and the matched-cost test both pass.
