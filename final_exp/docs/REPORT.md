# final_exp — updated Landlord-Repair randomized DRL vs the original paper

Generated by `experiments/summarize.py` from `results/*.json`.  The simulator, the 17 experiments and the exact conditions are described in the top-level `README.md`.

**Algorithms.** `Online-DRL` and `LL-RC` are the deterministic baselines of the paper.  `R3 only` is the randomized download threshold with the original Landlord admission.  `Randomized DRL` is R1 (fractional OGD) + R2 (randomized rounding) + R3 (randomized threshold) with the **updated Landlord-Repair** (Algorithm 3).  The previous stateless repair is reported as a separate variant in exp08.

**Data.** The paper's Google cluster traces, prepared with the paper's recipe (`data/prepare_google.py`): `google_dataset2_30.csv` (1 000 requests / 30 services), `google_dataset2_paper.csv` (1 000-row slice), `google.csv` (50 000 requests / 4 619 services).  SHARCNet is not reachable from this codespace.
